Honest check

Is it worth fixing?

Not every bike deserves a repair bill. Run through this checklist, or just roll the bike in and we will give you a straight answer for free.

  • The frame is straight and rust-free

    A sound frame is the one part that matters most. Almost everything else on a bike can be replaced for a reasonable price.

  • The repair costs less than half of a comparable new bike

    A €60 brake and gear job on a decent hybrid is money well spent. The same bill on a worn-out supermarket bike usually is not.

  • You actually like riding it

    A bike that fits you and feels right is worth keeping alive. Fit and comfort are expensive to buy new, cheap to maintain.

  • The frame is cracked, bent or deeply rusted

    Frame damage is a safety issue, not a repair ticket. We will not weld or bodge a cracked frame, and neither should anyone else.

  • Everything is worn at once

    Chain, cassette, chainrings, cables, tires and brake pads together can pass €200 in parts. At that point we will tell you honestly if the bike is done.
